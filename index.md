This is an initial draft of the 2027 programme. Workshops run in parallel on Monday 22 March and Wednesday 24 March. Tuesday 23 March is a plenary session on AI for Science.

**Monday 22 March**

- Multi-agent Systems
- Communication of Uncertainty — Tom Diethe, Thomas Meier, and Amr Mousa

**Tuesday 23 March**

- Plenary: AI for Science — Jessica Montgomery and Claire-Verity Hardgrove (tentative)

**Wednesday 24 March**

- Future of ML Publishing — Katherine Gorman and Hoel Kervadec
- Information, Entropy, and Learning — Michael Arbel, Joery de Vries, Aurélien Bibaut, and Francesco Locatello
