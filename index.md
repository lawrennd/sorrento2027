**Monday 22 March**

- AI for Science — Claire-Verity Hardgrove and Michael Arbel
- Communication of Uncertainty — Tom Diethe, Thomas Meier, and Amr Mousa

**Tuesday 23 March**

- Plenary: Public Interest AI — Jessica Montgomery

**Wednesday 24 March**

- Future of ML Publishing — Katherine Gorman, Hoel Kervadec, and Tegan Emerson
- Information, Entropy, and Learning — Joery de Vries, Aurélien Bibaut, and Francesco Locatello
